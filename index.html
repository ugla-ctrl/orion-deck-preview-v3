The problem

Event commitments become fixed before the information does

Iceland Eclipse 2026, IMXP's first major production, is the proof. Three findings from that event:

  • Accommodation was bought against forecast, not booked demand.
  • Partnership and vendor commitments locked in before softer sales were visible.
  • 56.8% of orders carried no attribution, across four systems.

The solution

Orion answers those three problems directly

Match commitments to demand. Surface decisions before deadlines. Carry context forward.

  • Match commitments to demand

    Compares bookings, inventory and spending exposure, live. Live today.

  • Surface decisions before deadlines

    Flags dependencies, owners and the last date to change course. Live today.

  • Carry context forward

    Links participant records and keeps decisions with their reasons and outcomes. In progress.
